log-completed-never — Habit

Why

any point a session would otherwise fire log-completed-liberal — substantial lens work that would normally earn a detailed entry

Action

do nothing; write no entry to any lens's completed log, regardless of how substantial the work was

Explanation

Pairs with log-session-never for sessions that aren't part of the project's real history, but can also apply independently — a session that wants the terse breadcrumb (orientation) without the detailed log-completed overhead (a quick fix, a one-off investigation).
